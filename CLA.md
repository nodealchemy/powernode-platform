## Why we ask for a CLA (plain English)

Powernode follows an **open-core** model. The platform, the `system`,
`marketing`, and `supply-chain` components are free and open source under the
MIT License, and they will stay that way. A separate **commercial business
extension** — covering things like multi-tenant SaaS operation, billing, and
enterprise features — is offered under a commercial license rather than MIT.

Because the same codebase is offered under more than one license, Node Alchemy
needs the legal right to **relicense and sublicense** the code it ships,
including code that contributors add. This CLA grants Node Alchemy that right.
It lets us keep the open-source project MIT-licensed for everyone while also
offering a commercially licensed edition to customers who need it — which is
what funds continued development of the free project.

**What this CLA does _not_ do:**

- It does **not** take away your ownership. You keep full copyright in your
  Contributions and may use, sell, or relicense them however you like elsewhere.
- It does **not** make your Contribution proprietary in the open-source project.
  Your Contribution remains available under the Project's open-source license.
- It does **not** obligate Node Alchemy to use or merge your Contribution, and
  it does **not** obligate you to provide support.

In short: you keep your rights, the open-source project stays open, and Node
Alchemy gains the flexibility to also offer a commercial edition.
